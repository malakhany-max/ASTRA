# Demo script: Zain, Day 90

**Target length:** 29 seconds. **All readings and events are simulated.**

| Time | Visual / action | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Introduce Zain and the mission-day label. | “Meet Zain, Day ninety of a simulated Mars mission.” |
| 4–9 s | Show the astronaut home view and one personal trend. | “ASTRA compares trusted readings with Zain’s own baseline.” |
| 9–14 s | Cut the link; show the calm Autonomous Mode badge. | “When the link drops, the Astronaut App stays in Autonomous Mode.” |
| 14–20 s | Show dosimeter readings, then a held suspect reading. | “Dosimeters measure radiation directly. Suspect data is held, not used.” |
| 20–24 s | Restore the link and show Ground Station updates. | “When the link returns, priorities reach the Ground Station.” |
| 24–29 s | Close on ASTRA title and simulated-data disclosure. | “ASTRA. Calm support, offline by design.” |

## Live walkthrough after the video

1. Open the astronaut app and explain a persistent change using its visible reason.
2. Trigger `radiation_spike`; show that the signal comes from a dosimeter and that shelter wording requires an approved local protocol.
3. Trigger `link_cut`; point out that onboard monitoring continues and Ground Station data is last known.
4. Trigger `link_restore`; show queued traffic syncing to the Ground Station.
5. Close by stating that every value is simulated and the product is decision support, not diagnosis.

Do not imply clinical validation, real astronaut telemetry, a live NASA protocol, or that a later symptom was caused by radiation. If the local voice assets are unavailable, use the text flow and do not suggest the laptop is synthesizing speech.
